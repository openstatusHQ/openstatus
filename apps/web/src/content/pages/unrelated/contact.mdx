Openstatus is built by a two-person team, so whoever answers you also wrote the
code. There is no ticket queue and no support tier — pick whichever channel
fits the question.

## Support

- **Email** — [ping@openstatus.dev](mailto:ping@openstatus.dev). Best for
  account, billing, and plan questions. We answer within one business day.
- **Discord** — [join the community](https://www.openstatus.dev/discord) for
  setup questions, monitor configuration, and quick answers from other users.
- **GitHub** — [openstatushq/openstatus](https://github.com/openstatushq/openstatus/issues)
  for bug reports and feature requests. Openstatus is open source, so the issue
  you file is the issue we work from.
- **Call** — [book 30 minutes](https://cal.com/team/openstatus/30min) with the
  founders for onboarding, migrations from another status page, or an
  architecture review before you roll out monitoring.

## Sales and enterprise

For volume pricing, invoicing, procurement paperwork, security questionnaires,
or a self-hosted deployment, email
[ping@openstatus.dev](mailto:ping@openstatus.dev) with the company name and what
you need. Vendor forms, SOC 2 evidence requests, and DPAs go to the same
address. See [pricing](/pricing) for the published plans and
[subprocessors](/subprocessors) for the vendors we rely on.

## Security

Report vulnerabilities to
[ping@openstatus.dev](mailto:ping@openstatus.dev) rather than in a public
issue — that is the contact published in
[/.well-known/security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) (RFC 9116). The
disclosure policy lives on
[GitHub](https://github.com/openstatusHQ/openstatus/security/policy) and the
rest of our posture is on the [security page](/security).

## Press and partnerships

Email [ping@openstatus.dev](mailto:ping@openstatus.dev) for logos, screenshots,
