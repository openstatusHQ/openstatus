You can now add monitors to your workspace without changing plans. A Monitor Pack adds **10 monitors for $15/month**, and you can stack up to 10 packs on the Starter, Pro and Scale plans.

Packs are billed monthly on top of your plan, alongside the existing add-ons. Your new limit applies immediately — the monitors you add count against your plan's allowance plus every pack you hold, so a Starter workspace with three packs can monitor up to 50 endpoints.

Removing packs works the same way in reverse. If you are still using more monitors than the smaller limit allows, we'll ask you to delete a few first rather than silently stopping checks.

####  Get Started 🚀
Head to Settings → Billing, find **Monitor Pack** under Add-ons, and choose how many packs you need. Need more than 100 extra monitors? [Book a call](https://openstatus.dev/cal) and we'll sort out a plan that fits.
