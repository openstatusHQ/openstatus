You can now pick a time zone for your status page. Timestamps, incident and maintenance updates, and the daily uptime bars all follow it, so your customers no longer have to convert from UTC.

Open your status page **Settings** and choose a zone in the **Time zone** card. Search by city or by short name like `PST` or `CET`. Pages default to UTC, and the setting is available on all paid plans.
