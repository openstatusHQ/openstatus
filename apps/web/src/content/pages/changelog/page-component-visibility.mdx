Monitor visibility is now set per status page. The same monitor can show its stats on an internal page and keep them private on your customer page.

Use the eye toggle next to each monitor component under [**Status Pages > Components**](https://app.openstatus.dev/status-pages/_/components). Existing pages keep their current visibility.

Read the [page components reference](/docs/reference/page-components#visibility) for the details.
