In this guide, you'll customise your status page's appearance and behaviour. You'll explore different display options, themes, and configuration settings to create a status page that matches your brand and communication style.

## Status page customisation

openstatus offers enhanced status page customisation with multiple themes and display options. Explore available themes at [themes.openstatus.dev](https://themes.openstatus.dev).

## Get started

Open your status page in the [dashboard](https://app.openstatus.dev/status-pages/_/edit). Configuration is split across two places:

- The **Components** page holds **Components Configuration** — how trackers render each component's data.
- The status page **Settings** hold General, Appearance, Custom Theme, Links, Locales, Page Access, Custom Domain, and the Danger Zone.

### View and configure status page

You can preview changes before committing to them. Click **View and configure status page** and you'll be forwarded to your status page with a floating button in the bottom right. Once you're done, click **Dashboard** to be forwarded back to your page, where you'll be asked to save the config before continuing.

### 1. Components configuration

The [**Components Configuration**](https://app.openstatus.dev/status-pages/_/components#components-configuration) card, on the **Components** page, controls two related things: how each component's **bars and cards** display its data, and the page-wide **uptime and history** view.

#### Bars and cards

These settings only apply to **monitor components** — the fields are disabled until your status page has at least one.

**Status source** — where the bars get their status from:
- **Monitor checks** — bars show your monitor's real results for each day.
- **Reports only** — bars only change when you post a status report or maintenance. Monitor results are hidden.

**Card shows** — only configurable if the status source is **Monitor checks**. Choose between **Time per status**, which shows how long the monitor was operational, degraded, down or in maintenance, **Number of requests**, which shows how many requests succeeded, degraded or failed in each region, or **Number of runs**, which counts each run once across all of the monitor's cloud regions and marks it down when at least half of them fail. Number of runs applies to HTTP, TCP, DNS, ICMP and gRPC monitors with cloud regions or a single private location; other monitors (several private locations only, heartbeat, UDP, SSL) keep counting each region's result. With **Reports only**, the card shows only the worst status of the day.

#### Uptime and history

**Show uptime** — with **Monitor checks**, uptime is calculated from the **time per status** (incidents and impact-weighted status reports; for monitors that run only from private locations, also failed probes), the **number of requests** or the **number of runs**, depending on what you've chosen for **Card shows**. With **Reports only**, it's calculated only from the duration of your status reports. See [Uptime calculation and shared values](/docs/concept/uptime-calculation-and-values) for details.

**History** — the number of days of history rendered on the bars. Choose between **30** or **45** days (defaults to **45**).

### 2. Appearance

Pick a theme in the **Style** field of the [**Appearance**](https://app.openstatus.dev/status-pages/_/edit#appearance) card in your status page settings. The shipped set is:

- `default` (openstatus)
- `default-rounded`
- `supabase`
- `github-contrast`
- `dracula`
- `passbolt`
- `gruvbox`
- `tomorrow`
- `probo`

Visit [themes.openstatus.dev](https://themes.openstatus.dev) to see the list of supported themes. You can also [create and contribute your own](/docs/guides/how-to-create-status-page-theme).

If you use AI agents, install the [`openstatus-theme` skill](https://github.com/openstatusHQ/skills/tree/main/skills/openstatus-theme) with `npx skills add openstatushq/skills --skill openstatus-theme` for guided help through palette design, accessibility checks, file scaffolding, and PR submission.

### 3. Links

Your status page header navigation:

**Homepage URL** — your logo will link to your own website.

**Contact URL** — if filled out, users will see a `Message` icon they can click to reach a contact page. This can also be an email client by starting the input with `mailto:` (e.g. `mailto:support@openstatus.dev`).

---

### 4. Locales

You can offer your status page in multiple languages. In the [**Locales**](https://app.openstatus.dev/status-pages/_/edit#locales) card of your status page settings:

1. Choose a **default locale** — the fallback language for visitors.
2. Enable the **locale switcher** to let visitors pick their preferred language.
3. Select which languages to make available.

See [How to translate your status page](/docs/guides/how-to-translate-status-page) for the supported list and how to contribute new translations.
